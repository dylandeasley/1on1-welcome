Your notes stay yours

  • Your 1on1 notes are for you and regional leadership. Other managers and admins can't read them.
  • Shared in confidence stays yours alone. Nobody else ever sees it, regional leadership included.
  • Each location is separate. Managers only ever see their own location's people.
  • FOH and BOH managers each see their own team. Nobody sees the other team's people.
  • Admins see counts only. How many 1on1s are happening, never what was said.
  • Only people an admin adds can sign in. Any other email never gets a login code.
